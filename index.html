AutoDJ & scheduling
Plan shows with recurring events and let AutoDJ cover the gaps automatically. The queue drains, refills and keeps playing, so your station never goes silent at 3 AM.
Autocue
Surcast trims leading and trailing silence, levels track loudness, and overlaps the tails. Flip the station to Autocue mode and the mix flows by itself, at one steady volume.

Self-host in minutes

terminal 
git clone --recurse-submodules https://github.com/Radio-Sur/surcast.git
cd surcast
docker compose up --build

That's the whole setup. Open the panel, create a station, upload music and you're on air!
